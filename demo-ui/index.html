WHY WE’RE BUILDING REVIZION

The little things
are the big things.

Picking up your drink. Finding your cup. Reaching for what’s yours. Everyday moments should feel like your own.

Our prototype brings together camera-based object detection, hand tracking, and a wristband with vibration motors. The goal is simple: useful guidance through touch, with less reliance on a screen.
